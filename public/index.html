How booking works

From quote to set-up in four steps

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send your date, venue, number of guests and the items you want.

  2. Get your quote

    We check that everything is free on your date and send a clear quote on WhatsApp.

  3. Pay the deposit

    Your date is held once the M-Pesa deposit arrives.

  4. We set up

    Our team delivers and sets everything up before your guests arrive.
